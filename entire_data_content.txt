Problem statement
Crop Removal
Remove crops with roots and trunk without causing any damage (e.g., jowar, maize, bajra).
Crop Waste Collection
Crop waste collection, such as wheat and rice, is currently handled by boring methods, which contribute to increased air pollution and a reduction in carbon percentage in the soil.
Sustainable Harvesting
The goal is to develop a solution that can efficiently remove crops while minimizing damage and addressing the issue of crop waste collection in an environmentally-friendly manner.
--- Slide 3 ---
Solution:
1
Crops are cut underground
At this stage, the first step is to remove the crop from the soil. Three types of mechanisms are used in this machine: a swinging mechanism, spring-connected teeth.
2
Collection the harvested crop
First, reduce the speed of each conveyor step. Next, pick up the cut and improperly arranged crop. We use torsion springs in this machine to collect the harvested crop. As well as use by left or slider.

Competitive Analysis
Existing Solutions
Combine harvesters and power tillers are currently used to harvest crops from above ground.
Innovative Approach
This project utilizes a new technology, such as a swinging mechanism, to remove crops with roots and trunks without causing damage.
Unique Features
The project can simultaneously harvest crops and collect crop waste, reducing air pollution and improving soil quality.
Competitive Advantage
This project has been granted a patent and there is currently no similar work product present in the agriculture industry.

Arrange meetings with farmers to understand their problems and explain the solution.
Understand the problem and then explain
Spend time understanding the farmers' problems in depth, and then clearly explain how the solution can address those issues.
